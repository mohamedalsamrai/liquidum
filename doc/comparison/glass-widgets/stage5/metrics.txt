Stage 5, bars (UINavigationBar's items, UIToolbar's items, the bottom
search field, the scroll-edge effect, large titles), 2026-11-11. UIKit = iOS
26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max (440 x 956, status bar 62, home indicator
34), 3 px/pt. RMS in levels (0-255) between UIKit's screen and Liquidum's,
per region: UIKit's bars from a UINavigationController page over a 44pt
pattern (the research catalog), Liquidum's from a GlassPage set up the same
way over the same pattern (example/lib/bars_comparison.dart).

Method
  UIKit's bars measured in a native catalog (simulator only): their layer
  trees and views (frames, fonts, colors), the glass archived, every frame
  of presses logged, and the scroll-edge effect's layers dumped with their
  filters' inputs; then screenshots over flat grays, stripes and the
  pattern, at set scroll offsets, light and dark.

UIKit's bars
  Navigation bar: 54pt under the status bar. Its glass: capsules 44pt tall
  at its top, 20pt from the screen's sides and 12pt apart, in one glass
  group (Stage 1's glass of that size). Items 36pt tall, 4pt in, 16pt
  apart: an icon its symbol's box plus 14, a label itself plus 24. Icons
  side by side share a capsule; a label or a prominent item has its own; a
  fixed space splits a group. A lone icon, and the back button, are 44pt
  circles. Title SF Semibold 17, centered; items SF Medium 17 (prominent:
  Semibold, white on the tint). Glyphs vibrant, as Stage 3's labels.
  Large title: SF Bold 34 (SF Pro Display) at x 20, in a 52pt row under the
  bar; it scrolls with the content and collapses into the bar's title once
  scrolled its row's height (the inline title rises 15pt into place).
  Toolbar: glass 48pt tall, 28pt from the sides and the bottom. A lone icon
  is a 48pt circle; a label its width plus 32. Items without spaces share
  a capsule (slots of the box plus 26, 4pt apart). With flexible spaces the
  first group sits at the start, the last at the end, one between in the
  middle.
  Bottom search field (a search controller on the root page): a 384 x 48
  capsule in the toolbar's place, the magnifier (label color, 2pt strokes)
  13pt into the field and the placeholder 41.67pt into it. Tapped: the
  navigation bar steps aside and the field shrinks for a 48pt close circle
  12pt away.
  Presses: Stage 3's flex interaction on each capsule (a 44 circle swells
  to 60, a 99 x 44 group to 115 x 51, whatever item is pressed). The
  pressed item fades: to 0.1 of its contrast in the navigation bar, about
  0.6 in the toolbar; the others on the glass keep about 0.87.
  Scroll-edge effect: where a scroll view's content is under a bar (at the
  top once scrolled; at the bottom while there is more below). A soft step
  at the bar's glass (the navigation bar's bottom, 10pt into the toolbar):
  m = Phi(d / 18pt). Under it a Gaussian blur of up to 1.72pt (through
  1.25 m - 0.25; not at the bottom, and not while a large title shows),
  and either black at 0.25 or a wash toward the page's background. With
  the system light: black over content darker than a mean luma of 0.675,
  else a wash of 0.85 (0.8 fitted). With it dark: a wash of 0.85, 0.6 or
  0.3 as the content is darker or lighter (steps near 0.35 and 0.85). The
  title turns white with the black; the status bar follows.

Open
  - Dark glass over dimmed content is a little grayer than UIKit's (dark
    scrolled blocks, 29 to 36): Stage 1's dark look.
  - With a large title at 30pt UIKit washed the bottom light over dark
    content (83); at 0 and once collapsed it does not, as Liquidum.
  - Dark mode over white: UIKit's bottom wash was 0.1 where its top's was
    0.3 (36).
  - Icons are the app's IconData; UIKit's are SF symbols.
  - The effects' fade timings, the search field's animation and keyboard
    gap are not measured; push and pop transitions are Stage 6's.
