Stage 4, lens controls (UISwitch, UISlider, UISegmentedControl, iOS 26:
their thumb or selection is the liquid lens), 2026-11-11. UIKit = iOS 26.5
simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max, 3 px/pt. RMS in levels (0-255) between
UIKit's and Liquidum's at the same places over the same backgrounds
(example/lib/comparison.dart with shapes=controls: UIKit's controls in a
platform view, example/lib/native_glass.dart; Liquidum's GlassSwitch,
GlassSlider and GlassSegmentedControl).

Method
  UIKit's controls measured in a native catalog (simulator only): their
  layer trees and frames, the lens's glass archived while held
  (NSKeyedArchiver: the filters' inputs), and every frame of taps, holds
  and drags logged; springs fitted to those frames. Liquidum's lens is the
  tab bar's, extracted (lib/src/lens: LiquidLens, LensLift, LensFlex; the
  bar's shader math shared in shaders/lens_material.glsl, the bar's output
  unchanged to the level).

UISwitch
  63 x 28, radius 14. Thumb 37 x 24, inset 2, travel 22pt; an opaque white
  capsule at rest. Lifted (touched): the "small" lens, 58 x 38.33,
  refraction -h/2 over 0.18 h, highlight 0.25, shadow h/4 at 0.18; it
  lifts on touch (k ~800, c ~31), even for a tap (about 0.15s), and falls
  back with no hang. Travel: spring 452 / 44.8; a drag moves the thumb 1:1
  (31% rubber band past an end) and toggles on release past the middle.
  Track: off (60, 60, 67) at 0.30 light / (235, 235, 245) at 0.30 dark,
  on systemGreen (52, 199, 89) / (48, 209, 88) or onTintColor, swept
  critically damped (omega 7). Disabled: half alpha, as one layer.
UISlider
  34 tall; track 6 (radius 3) at y 14, the max track black at 0.10 / white
  at 0.10, the min track systemBlue (0, 136, 255) / (0, 145, 255) or
  minimumTrackTintColor. Thumb 37 x 24 at y 5, x = value x (width - 37),
  shadow 0.14 / blur 5 / y 1; lifted 57.33 x 37 into the small lens. A
  touch on the thumb lifts it; a touch on the track does nothing.
UISegmentedControl
  32 tall, radius 16, equal segments; the selection inset 2, white (dark:
  (193, 193, 204) at 0.47) or selectedSegmentTintColor. Track (115, 115,
  123) at 0.12 / (117, 117, 130) at 0.24. Labels SF 13pt, regular, the
  selected one medium (UIKit's G3 grade: its glyphs are 3.5% larger than
  Flutter's 13pt, so Liquidum draws 13.45pt), above the lens. A touch on
  an unselected segment never lifts: on release the segment under the
  finger is selected and the selection slides there (235 / 26). A touch on
  the selected segment lifts it into the tab bar's lens (lifted outsets
  12 / 8, spring 612 / 49); it follows the finger 1:1, the label under it
  turns medium, and on release it snaps to the segment under it.

  The worst frames are mid-motion. UIKit's lifted lens trails the finger
  (the slider's 25pt behind while held, 11pt after release; the others
  alike); Liquidum's stays under it (this package's rule: true 1:1), so
  held and settled slider frames compare thumbs at different places.

Open
  - The lifted small lens: UIKit's interior magnifies the backdrop evenly
    (the slider's track 1.6x thicker); Liquidum's refraction gathers near
    the rim.
  - The switch's lift spring is fitted from frames (not in UIKit's
    settings); each control's lifted outsets are constants.
  - Haptics are out of scope.
