Stage 6, part 1: pull-down menus (UIButton showsMenuAsPrimaryAction) and
UIKit's liquid morph, 2026-11-12. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17
Pro Max (440 x 956), 3 px/pt. Liquidum: GlassButton(menu: [GlassMenuItem])
in example/lib/menus_comparison.dart, laid out as the research catalog's
menus page (glass buttons over the 44pt pattern).

Method
  UIKit's menu measured in a native catalog (simulator only): its views
  (frames, fonts, colors), its glass archived, and every frame of its two
  morph views logged (_UIMagicMorphView: scale, center, bounds, crossfade);
  springs fitted to those frames. Screen video at 60fps (simctl
  recordVideo) for the look of the morph; screenshots for the rest.

UIKit's menu
  Platter: 250pt of large regular glass (exactly Stage 1's inputs for its
  size), continuous corners of 34pt, anchored at the button's top-left
  corner and covering it; from its right edge near the screen's right; up
  from its bottom edge, rows reversed, near the bottom.
  Rows 42pt, 10pt from the top and bottom; groups 21pt apart with a 1pt
  hairline (black 0.08) 24pt in. SF Regular 17, black 0.96 (white in
  dark), not vibrant; destructive systemRed. Label 28pt in (79 with
  icons); icon centered 55pt in; checkmark 21pt in; submenu chevron 214pt
  in. Highlight: a capsule 10pt in from the sides.
  Opens on touch down; a finger that slides from the button onto a row and
  lifts chooses it (the platter leans toward it and glows: flex press).
  Submenu: its own platter over the menu, a 62pt header (label semibold, a
  chevron turned down) centered on its row, the menu receding to 0.97 and
  dimming; it grows out of the row's highlight (omega 15.86, zeta 0.853).
Morph (fitted, rms under 0.002 in scale)
  Two shapes melting as one glass: the button (scale 1 -> 0.25, a quarter
  of the way toward the menu's center, capsule -> circle) and the menu (a
  circle half the button's height across at its center -> its rect), both
  on one progress spring: omega 18.1, zeta 0.75 opening; 12.85, 0.80
  closing. The menu's vertical center has its own spring with a kick along
  its growth (open omega 18, zeta 0.6, 10 x the height gained pt/s; close
  16.3, 0.82, 520 + 5.8 x the height lost), so it sags past its place and
  settles. Content crossfades through a blur; closing, it blurs away
  within ~0.05s.

Results
  Open menu (1-menu.jpg; 7 rows with icons, a checkmark, a submenu row, a
  destructive row): rows within 1pt of UIKit's (text 224.0 vs 225.0pt);
  the glass off the text within 5.9 levels RMS; dark (2-menu-dark.jpg)
  4.9 levels.
  Morph, frame by frame against UIKit's trace (2 rows): the menu's width,
  height and horizontal travel within 1 to 3pt at every frame, opening and
  closing; its sag on closing 18pt (UIKit 17.7pt). 3-open.jpg, 4-close.jpg:
  time-aligned video frames (Liquidum's debug build on the simulator draws
  ~20fps, so its in-between frames are coarser).
  Submenu (5-submenu.jpg), drag to choose (6-drag.jpg): same layout and
  sequence.
  Android emulator, profile mode: the open menu rasters in 15-20ms a frame
  while it moves, 4-10ms while the blob is small; at rest it draws nothing.

Open
  - UIKit nudges the menu 1 to 5pt off the button's corner in some cases
    (7 rows: 18,199 for a button at 20,200; bottom: 15); Liquidum anchors
    exactly.
  - The gaussian blur radius of UIKit's crossfade is not readable from its
    layers; Liquidum's (8pt opening, 12pt closing) is fitted by eye.
  - The source's tilt ("PivotView") during the morph is not copied.
  - The highlight is a light wash, not UIKit's vibrant white fill.
  - The edit menu and the date picker overlay are the next parts of
    Stage 6.

Part 2: bar item menus and the back button's history, 2026-11-12.
UIKit = a UINavigationController of three pages ("First", "Second",
"Third"), the last with an ellipsis UIBarButtonItem with a menu (the
catalog's menusnav page); Liquidum = three GlassPages pushed the same way
(menus_comparison.dart menusnav).

UIKit's
  An item with a menu: opens on touch down out of the item's platter (a
  44pt circle), the same morph and springs as a button's (fitted: omega
  18.05, zeta 0.751); the menu at the bar's top (62), its right edge on
  the platter's (UIKit's 3.7pt nudge again).
  Back button: held still 0.595s (touch down to the morph), the history
  opens out of it: an ordinary menu of the pages under this one, nearest
  first ("Second", "First"), 42pt rows, at the button's corner (20,62).
  Lifting the finger on a row, even where it went down, goes back to that
  page (the back button sits under the first row: "Second"). The morph
  starts from the platter swollen by the press (scale 1.12 to 1.33).

Results (7-bar-menus.jpg, UIKit left): both menus' rows, glass and
  corners as UIKit's; the item's menu 3.7pt left of UIKit's (its nudge).
  Tests: the item's menu at the platter's right edge and the bar's top;
  the history lists the pages nearest first, at the back button's corner,
  and lifting on a row (moved or not) goes back to it.

Open
  - The morph starts from the platter at rest, not swollen by the press.
  - Liquidum's history lists GlassPages (by their titles) in the same
    Navigator; pages without a title are left out.

Part 3: context menus, 2026-11-12.
UIKit = UIContextMenuInteraction on the catalog's menus page: a 140pt
tile (continuous corners 16) at 40,280 and one at 260,640, a white label
(251.67 x 44) at 20,450 and a clear one (110 x 44) at 300,530; items
Copy, Share, -, Delete. Liquidum = GlassContextMenu around the same views
(menus_comparison.dart menus). Per-frame layer traces of warm opens and
closes, screenshots light and dark.

UIKit's
  Press: 0.18s after touch down the view is lifted into a platter that
  grows while the finger rests: its longer side +15.7pt on a critically
  damped spring (omega 5.77), the same points for any size (152.4 for the
  140 tile and 264.1 for the 251.67 label at 0.74s), about its center.
  Open: 0.80s after touch down. The view lifts to 1.15x, or 26pt longer
  on its longer side when that is less (the label: 277.67 x 48.67), kept
  20pt from the screen's sides (the label moves 13pt right); one spring,
  omega 21, zeta 0.8 (closing 20.8, 0.8). The screen dims under a plain
  overlay of (0.086, 0.082, 0.165) at 0.21, light and dark alike, no
  blur (omega 20.7 in, 16.1 out). The lifted view casts a shadow from its
  opaque pixels: black, 0.27, sigma 18.5pt, no offset (a clear label
  casts none and gets no platter behind it). Its corners are continuous,
  13pt at the view's own size (14.3 on the label).
  The menu: the same glass and rows as a pull-down's, 20pt from the view
  on the side with more room (the clear label at y 552 goes above with
  room below), from the view's left edge, or its right edge when it would
  cross the screen's; rows in their order either way. It grows with the
  liquid morph out of the view's center, from 0.4 of the view (each side
  at most the menu's shorter side: 56 x 56 for the tile, 66.8 x 17.6 for
  the label), no source glass; its center travels with the progress, its
  size grows geometrically a little ahead (q = p + 0.32 p(1 - p); fitted
  over three views open and close, 0.7pt rms). Closing reverses it.
  After the open the finger can slide onto a row and lift to choose it;
  lifting elsewhere leaves the menu open; a tap outside (or on the view)
  closes it.

Results (8-context.jpg, UIKit left)
  Screens (mean |difference|, 0-255): the dim 0.3 to 0.9; the lifted
  tiles 0.4 to 0.5; the shadow band around them 2.2 to 2.6; the label 5.3
  (its text rendering; its corners within 0.4pt); the menus 8 to 10
  (2.3 to 3.5 between rows, 10 along the top edge, see Open).
  Motion against the traces (60fps, same touch): the lifted view within
  0.4pt; the menu's width within 3.6pt (rms 0.7), height 2.4 (0.5),
  center 5.2 (0.8) opening; closing 3.5, 2.2 and 1.5.
  Android (emulator, profile): later opens drop 0 to 1 frames; the very
  first open after installing compiles once (252ms), not after restarts.
  Tests: places and sizes of the three views, the lift, the drag onto a
  row, a short tap left to the view, sliding away cancels.

Open
  - The menu's glass over sharp text (the white label under its top)
    shows the text refracted along its rim; UIKit's shows it frosted.
  - The view crossfades from its pressed to its lifted snapshot in UIKit
    (it looks see-through for a frame or two); Liquidum draws one view.
  - The platter shadow's fade during the lift is not measured (Liquidum
    fades it in with the lift).


Part 4: the bars between two pages (push and pop), 2026-11-12.
UIKit = a UINavigationController whose page has a "Push" button pushing a
second page (the catalog's barsroot pushbtn=1): "List" with "+" -> "Detail"
with a back button and "... share" (push1); "List" with [Edit] [+] ->
"Detail" with "..." -> "Detail" (push2); "List" with "+" -> "Detail" with a
toolbar [trash] ~ [share] ~ [Done] (push3). Per-frame layer traces and
screen videos. Liquidum = GlassPages pushed with CupertinoPageRoute
(bars_comparison.dart pushbtn=1).

UIKit's
  The bar stays; the pages slide under it (the new page from 440 to 0, the
  old to -132). Titles ride with their pages: the old one fades out in
  about 0.1s pushing, at once popping; the one coming shows.
  Platters pair from the bar's ends inward.
  - The same (same items): no change at all (Detail -> Detail).
  - Different items: its width springs to the other's (omega 15.1, zeta
    0.75, from 0.075s), its items crossfade (omega 15.1 critically damped)
    through a blur, and it swells and settles (scale 1 + 0.5 e^-zwt
    sin(w_d t), omega 12.7, zeta 0.53, from 0.035s; peak 1.23). Pop: the
    same laws.
  - On the page going only: melts into its neighbor (toward the bar's
    end), to a circle half its height across at the neighbor's center, on
    the width's spring; its items fade at once.
  - On the page coming only: grows out of its neighbor's center from a
    circle 0.2 of its height, from 0.21s (omega 20, zeta 0.7).
  - The back button on one page only: materializes in place (omega 13.5,
    critically damped, from 0.044s; its chevron 12.4, 0.91, from 0.1s), or
    goes in about 0.15s (28.8, 0.93).
  - A toolbar on one page only: stays in place and materializes (about
    omega 25, critically damped, 0.2s) or goes as fast as the back button.

Results (9-bar-transitions.jpg, UIKit left; bar strips at 0.03, 0.08,
  0.13, 0.2, 0.3, 0.6s after the push or pop)
  Against the traces, frame by frame (widget-test probe, 60fps): the
  morphing platter 370.3,58.0 53.9x52.1 at 0.1s (UIKit 370.3,58.0
  53.9x52.0), 352.7,57.1 74.0x53.9 at 0.15s (351.9,57.0 75.1x54.1); popping
  314.4,58.9 112.5x50.2 at 0.083s (313.4,58.7 114.1x50.7); the melted
  platter a 22x22 circle at 387,73 (UIKit's exactly); the one growing back
  out an 8.8pt circle at 393.6,79.6 at first (UIKit's exactly).
  The page on top draws both pages' platters, fixed on the screen (an
  offset, no layer, so the glass still shares its reads); a pop hands the
  last of its springs to the page under it as it goes.
  Tests: the morph at UIKit's places, fixed while the page slides, the
  melt and growth, the same platters still, a swipe back driving it and
  given up, a toolbar on one page.

Open
  - The pages slide on Flutter's CupertinoPageRoute: slower at first than
    UIKit's (at 0.1s, 240 to go against UIKit's 120); Liquidum does not
    own the route.
  - UIKit blurs the items as they crossfade; Liquidum's labels are drawn
    by the glass, out of a blur's reach, so they crossfade sharp.
  - Two different toolbars crossfade (one goes, the other comes) instead
    of morphing platter by platter (not measured).
  - The bottom search field and large titles are not part of the
    transition yet (they ride with their pages).

Part 5: the edit menu (UIEditMenuInteraction), 2026-11-14.

UIKit (iOS 26.5, iPhone 17 Pro Max; a text view with a word selected, and
a view with UIEditMenuInteraction and its own actions)
  The menu: one small regular glass capsule, 46pt tall, r 23, 4pt in; each
  action 16pt either side of its label (SF 15, vibrant), side by side; a
  1 x 20 line between actions. Actions that do not fit across the screen
  less 20pt a side give way to a 36pt circle (a faint fill) with a
  chevron, 4pt from the end. Labels only (icons for actions without one).
  Its glass keeps the system's appearance with large glass's fixed face
  (light: black 0.5, white 1.03, white fill 0.4; dark: 0.2, 0.6, black
  fill 0.4; archived), small as it is; its other inputs follow its 46pt.
  Placement: 15pt below the selection (the text system's own menu too),
  above it (15pt over it) only when it does not fit below; its list (the
  glass less 8pt, 6 with the chevron) centered on the selection's middle,
  20pt from the screen's sides.
  Showing: scale 0.2 to 1 about the selection's middle on the edge facing
  it, and opacity 0 to 1, on one spring: omega 22.8, zeta 0.85 (rms 0.0002
  of the scale); hiding the same way back, omega 19.1, zeta 0.85, as soon
  as an action is chosen.
  Pressed: the glass swells as interactive glass (1.071 for 223 x 46, the
  Stage 3 law); the held action gets a capsule 38pt tall across its width
  (tertiary fill), and the lines beside it go.
  The chevron: the capsule melts into the full menu of every action with
  part 1's liquid morph (fit omega 17.0, zeta 0.75), the capsule narrowing
  into its chevron's end, the menu growing from there: 250 wide, its top
  at the capsule's top, its right edge at the capsule's; Cut, Copy and
  Paste as tiles across its top (78 x 77 each for three: icon centered 29pt
  down, SF Medium 12 label from 43.67pt), a hairline at their bottom and
  11pt to the rows; rows without a checkmark column have their icon at 40
  and label at 64 (part 1's 55 and 79 are with one). A choice or a tap
  outside shrinks it into the selection (part 1's close spring); the
  capsule does not come back.

Liquidum: `GlassEditMenu`
  `CupertinoTextField(contextMenuBuilder: GlassEditMenu.builder)`, or
  `GlassEditMenu(anchors:, items:)` through a ContextMenuController, or
  `GlassEditMenu.buttonItems(anchors:, buttonItems:)` for Flutter's text
  actions (SelectableRegion). Flutter removes a context menu at once; a
  hidden copy of the menu above it takes its place in that same frame and
  hides on UIKit's spring.

Results (10-edit-menu.jpg, UIKit left; the comparison page wrapped in a
  GlassBackdrop, as GlassPage is)
  The capsule's glass: light over the white text view 252 / 252; dark over
  the black text view 30 / 30; light over the blue box 149,220,255 /
  147,220,255; dark over it 0,70,134 / 0,68,136 (0-3 levels). Labels and
  lines at UIKit's places (SF 15 at -0.23 tracking gives UIKit's widths);
  the dark vibrant labels 178,249,255 both.
  Showing, against the trace (widget-test probe): 0.454 of the width at
  47ms, 0.838 at 112ms (UIKit's). The full menu as UIKit's (its glass is
  part 1's).
  Tests: the place below and above, 20pt from the side, the growth, a
  choice and the hiding copy (no frame without the menu), the chevron's
  full menu and a tap outside it, a CupertinoTextField's Copy.

Open
  - Flutter has no Replace… or Translate; its text actions are Cut, Copy,
    Paste, Select All, Look Up, Search Web, Share and Scan Text.
  - The tiles' press highlight and the menu's press-drag across actions
    (UIKit slides the highlight along under the finger) are not measured.
  - Not checked on Android.
