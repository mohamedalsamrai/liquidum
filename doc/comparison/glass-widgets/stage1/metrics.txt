Stage 1, the glass material (UIGlassEffect), 2026-10-25. UIKit = iOS 26.5
simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max, 3 px/pt. RMS in levels (0-255) between UIKit's
glass and Liquidum's Glass at the same rects over the same backgrounds
(example/lib/comparison.dart with shapes=<set>: UIKit's in a platform view,
example/lib/native_glass.dart). "middle": more than 3pt inside the outline;
"edge": the 3pt inside it; "around": the 30pt outside it (neighbours'
shadows included in the dense sets).

Method
  UIKit's own inputs: each UIGlassEffect's glass group archived
  (NSKeyedArchiver) and its glassBackground filter, CASDF effects and
  vibrant matrices decoded, at 30 to 400pt, regular and clear, tinted, light
  and dark, over white, black and color. The effect of single inputs was
  isolated by overriding them on the live layers (simulator only), e.g. the
  frost alone (refraction, face, bleed and shadow off) over stripes.

The material is the tab bar's
  A 300x62 capsule of regular glass has exactly the tab bar platter's
  inputs: the bar's measured glass is UIGlassEffect's. Liquidum's Glass now
  draws it with the bar's code (shaders/glass_material.glsl, shared by
  backdrop.frag and glass.frag; the bar is pixel-identical before and after,
  9 scenes, 0 pixels differing).

UIKit's laws, by the shape's short side h (points), any corner radius
  regular: inner refraction 0.8h (max 60) at 0.25h deep (max 20); outer
    refraction 0.2h at h/8; frost: heavy over the middle line (h/2), blur
    radius (h/2 + 4) / 21 (min 4/3, max 4); shadow opacity 0.5 - 5t light,
    0.4 - 3t dark, t = clamp(h - 48, 0, 112) / 2240 (0.25 from 160pt).
  clear: inner refraction h (max 60) at 0.36h (max 20); face fixed (black
    0.075, white 1.15, chroma 1.06, no fill), no shadow, no bleed, blur
    radius 1.
  up to 64pt: the face follows the backdrop's luma and the appearance
    switches with it, as the bar's (GlassAppearance), sampled over the glass
    grown by 0.2h.
  over 64pt ("large"): the system's appearance only; face fixed (light:
    black 0.5, white 1.03, white fill 0.4, shadow fill 0.12; dark: 0.2, 0.6,
    black fill 0.4); a bleed mixed over the glass at (h - 64) / 192 light,
    / 120 dark (max at 160pt: 0.5, 0.8); the shadow darkened by its
    vibrancy contribution (h - 64) / 96 (max 1): + 0.15 of it.
  everything stops growing at 160pt but the frost's depth (h/2).
  corners: Apple's continuous curve. Measured from UIKit's silhouettes (rim
    hidden): circles are exact circles; capsules and rounded rects are
    pulled in where an arc meets a straight side, by up to 0.017 r, over
    0.52 r of the side and about 30 degrees of the arc (none at a capsule's
    end). Modeled as an inset of the distance field (continuousInset), within
    0.1pt. Outer band (the outline's last point) over gray, before/after:
    62pt 17.2 -> 3.9, 100pt 28.9 -> 5.4, 120pt r40 30.2 -> 5.3.
  refraction follows the shape's own distance gradient: no ovalization
    (the bar's 0.5 belongs to its lens's warp). Clear over stripes 42-59 ->
    7-8 levels inside.

Frost by height (UIKit's frost alone, fitted over 8pt stripes)
  light sigma / heavy sigma / ramp power: 56pt 0.92/2.63/1.13, 62pt
  0.93/2.99/1.06, 66pt 0.97/3.32/1.03, 80pt 1.15/4.36/0.68, 90pt
  1.36/4.12/0.51, 160pt 2.66/10.5/0.42. Liquidum keeps the bar's fit at
  62pt and below, scales the heavy blur and the ramp by these ratios above,
  and adds the light blur in quadrature. Stripe contrast UIKit/Liquidum at
  3, 8, 13pt deep: within 0.83-1.36 up to 90pt (was 0.30-0.93).
  Impeller blurs less than asked below sigma 3 (0.75 at 1, 0.90 at 2,
  measured over stripes); clear glass asks 1.88 for UIKit's 1.67.

Bleed (large glass)
  Linear: UIKit's glass = no-bleed glass + opacity x (bleed - no-bleed),
  within 0.7 levels. Its bleed layer is not a blur of the backdrop (a
  black/white split stays sharp; with its blur radius at 0 it is min(G +
  12/255, 0.969) over gray), and over color is not yet understood. Modeled
  as an affine color matrix of the glass, fitted at 100-400pt: the bleed
  alone, light within 2-4 levels, dark 1-2.

Results (middle / edge, levels)
  over color blocks: 30-64pt 0.8-1.8 / 3.4-7.1; 66-160pt 2.9-4.7 / 9-14;
    panels 200-400pt 5.2-5.4 / 12-14; clear 1.1-2.9 / 2.5-3.9
  over gray: 0.1-0.9 (2.5 for two capsules whose UIKit reading includes the
    shadows of the shapes above them)
  over dark: 0.1-1.2 / 0.5-4.3
  tints (blue, red, half blue, clear blue): 0.2-0.6 / 1.6-2.9
  shadows, apart over white: all within 1 level at 1-44pt below.
  clear over 8pt stripes: 7.2-7.8 / 6-13 (the outermost 2pt brighter than
    UIKit's, which dims it near the edge).

Open
  - Large glass's bleed: its real composition (inputs BleedAmount,
    BleedHeight, BleedDistance0/1, DarkenBlend), for panels (Stage 7).
  - Panels keep slightly less chroma and texture than UIKit's (about 5
    levels inside).
  - The rim's outermost point on circles (7.7 levels over gray) and clear
    glass's first 2pt.
  - The interactive press (isInteractive: swell, stretch, fingertip glow)
    moves to Stage 3, with the glass buttons, which use the same flex
    interaction.
  - The bar's own capsules could take the continuous corners and the
    platter's refraction its gradient without ovalization; not changed yet
    (the bar is kept identical), to compare with UIKit's bar first.
