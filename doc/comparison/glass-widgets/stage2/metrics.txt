Stage 2, glass groups (UIGlassContainerEffect) and glass appearing and
leaving, 2026-11-09. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max, 3 px/pt.
RMS in levels (0-255) between UIKit's glass and Liquidum's at the same
rects over the same backgrounds (example/lib/comparison.dart with
shapes=<set>: UIKit's in UIGlassContainerEffects in a platform view,
example/lib/native_glass.dart; Liquidum's in GlassGroups). "inside": the
group's melted outline; "edge": its 3pt inside the outline; "middle": more
than 3pt in; "necks": the outline outside every member; "around": 30pt
outside (shadow).

Method
  UIKit's containers measured in a native catalog (simulator only): their
  layer trees and archived glass groups (NSKeyedArchiver); silhouettes over
  black with the rim hidden (two capsules end to end at gaps 0-32 and
  spacings 10, 20, 40; circles; capsules stacked; rows, triangles and 2x2
  grids); and, for glass appearing and leaving, the presentation values of
  every glass filter input on every frame.

UIKit's container
  One glass group for the container: one backdrop with UIKit's glass filter
  and one SDF layer, with an element per glass view (the views draw no
  glass of their own). The container's spacing is the SDF layer's
  smoothness (points); UIKit's default spacing is 0. Regular and clear
  glass in one container are two glass groups: they do not melt together.
  A tint stays with its glass (a material of its own over that glass's
  shape).

The union (silhouettes; mean outline offset 0.02-0.06pt over 25 layouts of
arcs meeting arcs, 0.11pt for a triangle at spacing 40)
  f = min over every pair (i, j) of smin(d_i, d_j, k_ij), and over each d_i
  smin(a, b, k) = min(a, b) - h^2 k / 4, h = max(k - |a - b|, 0) / k
  k_ij = spacing x (1 - n_i . n_j) / 2, n = each shape's outward direction
  d_i: each shape's distance, with Apple's continuous corners (Stage 1).
  Surfaces that face each other melt fully, perpendicular ones half, side
  by side not at all. Two capsules end to end join when their gap is under
  half the spacing; further apart (up to the spacing) their ends still
  lean toward each other (spacing 20, gap 12: 1.55pt each).
  Rejected: plain smooth minima (quadratic, cubic, exponential, circular:
  1.1-1.3pt), the angle weighting the pull instead of k (0.4-0.5pt), and
  folding each shape into the running union (order-dependent; it fills a
  triangle's hole: 0.25 and 0.89pt against 0.06 and 0.11pt). Blending only
  the two nearest shapes gives the same outlines as all pairs here.
  Inside a blend the field's gradient is shorter than 1 and vanishes at a
  neck's saddle; Liquidum refracts along it unnormalized (normalized, the
  refraction flips across a neck's middle: a seam UIKit does not have) and
  measures the rim by field / gradient length (UIKit's rim keeps its width
  in a notch). Neck RMS over blocks 40-47 -> 4-8 levels, over dark 7-15 ->
  0.2-1.7.

The group's inputs (archived): one set for all its glass
  From its smallest glass (short side): inner refraction and its depth,
  frost depth. From its largest: outer refraction, blur radius, bleed,
  shadow, the large-glass face. A small group turns light or dark as one,
  from what is behind all its glass: two 48pt circles, one over black and
  one over white, share one face (black 0.366, white 0.966, white fill
  0.49), where alone they take dark and light faces. Liquidum samples each
  glass's own area and averages them by area.
  A tint reaches past its glass into a neck: 10/50/90% at 7.45/5.15/2.66pt
  outside its outline, the same at spacing 20 and 40.

Appearing and leaving (UIView.animate { view.effect = glass / nil })
  The glass keeps its size; every glass filter input scales by one progress
  p, the animation's own curve (ease in and out over the duration asked):
  blur radius, frost opacities and depth, refractions and their depths,
  face opacity and matrix (black x p, white 1 + (w - 1) p, fill x p),
  shadow opacity, rim. In a container, appearing glass joins the group's
  outline at once (its neck is there at full strength from the first
  frame) and its own outline fades in; leaving glass parts from the group
  at once and fades out alone. Glass appearing shows UIKit's default look
  and adapts to what is behind it afterwards.
  Liquidum (Glass.visible, 0.5s ease in and out): the same scaling, per
  pixel within each glass's own outline; the heavy blur's sigma follows the
  group's most materialized glass. Progress of a lone 150x62 capsule over
  color blocks (least squares from backdrop to finished glass, 10x slow
  motion): 2% to 97% in 0.36s (UIKit) and 0.34s (Liquidum), within 0.05 of
  each other throughout; leaving, 0.37s and 0.38s.

Lone glass (Stage 1) is unchanged: against Stage 1's build, the sizes,
clears, tints, heights, apart, big and big2 sets differ by 0.005-0.27 RMS
(glass up to 62pt: at most a few pixels by 1 level; larger glass 1-2
levels where Impeller's heavy blur rounds differently over the group's
bounds, and up to 38 on a few edge pixels where a large corner's arc meets
its side, the frost now following the continuous corner). The tab bar is
pixel-identical.

Open
  - Where flat faces bridge and turn into corners, UIKit's bridge ends
    sooner: notch tips 2-4.6pt deeper than the model's (60pt squares,
    radius 16 and 4, gaps 2-9; stacked capsules 2.0-2.4pt). The pull there
    depends on more than the surfaces' angle; seen as a 2x2 grid's hole (a
    four-pointed star in UIKit, a diamond here) and the ends of a bridge
    between stacked bars. Arcs meeting arcs (rows of capsules and circles)
    fit within 0.06pt.
  - Over gray, UIKit's groups of 48-50pt glass are 3 levels lighter inside
    than its lone glass of that size (208 against 205); Liquidum's are
    205. 62pt groups match exactly.
  - The rim along a regular neck's curved notch is a little fainter than
    UIKit's.
