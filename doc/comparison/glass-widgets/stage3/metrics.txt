Stage 3, glass buttons (UIButton.Configuration.glass() and kin) and the
interactive press (UIGlassEffect.isInteractive), 2026-11-10. UIKit = iOS
26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max, 3 px/pt. RMS in levels (0-255) between
UIKit's and Liquidum's at the same places over the same backgrounds
(example/lib/comparison.dart with shapes=buttons: UIKit's UIButtons in a
platform view, example/lib/native_glass.dart; Liquidum's GlassButtons and
Glass(interactive: true)).

Method
  UIKit's buttons measured in a native catalog (simulator only): their
  layer trees, their glass groups archived (NSKeyedArchiver: the filters'
  inputs and color matrices), UIKit's flex interaction settings read at
  run time (_UIFlexInteractionSettingsDomain), and every frame of presses,
  holds and drags logged (each layer's transform, opacity and frame).

UIKit's glass button
  The same glass group as UIGlassEffect's (one element, the button's
  capsule) behind the button's content; prominent glass adds a tint
  material over the shape (the tinted glass's). The title and image are
  drawn through a vibrantColorMatrix: 1.5 x the color less 1.4 x its luma,
  offset 0.9, the same on every style.
  Sizes (buttonSize): medium (the default) 38.33pt tall, insets 7 / 12, SF
  17pt; large 54.33pt, 15 / 20, 17pt; small and mini 32pt, 5 / 10, 15pt.
  An image alone: a circle of 34.33, 50.33 or 28pt; the symbol's box 23 x
  21.67pt (20.33 x 19 small), 6pt before the title.
  Label colors: the label color (black on light glass), white on prominent
  glass, the tertiary label color (60, 60, 67 at 0.3) when disabled.
  Prominent clear glass: a gray material over clear glass (0.22 of its luma
  plus 0.25, at 0.16), whatever the tint. Disabled prominent glass: no tint,
  a material that pulls the glass's luma to about half (color less 0.93 of
  its luma plus 0.5, at 0.45). Plain glass ignores the tint.

The label
  Liquidum draws the label as UIKit's matrix over the glass under it: 0.69
  x the matrix (its offset left out) plus 0.9 x the label's color, the
  glyphs as a mask (fitted to UIKit's labels over color blocks: black
  labels within 2 to 9 levels, white ones within 5 to 13). Pressed, UIKit's
  label shows half as strongly, under the press's glow.

The press (UIKit's _UIFlexInteraction)
  Swell: the glass and its content scale uniformly by (length + lift) /
  length. Glass up to 110pt long, any aspect, is "ultra small": lift 16pt,
  glow 1, wash 1. Longer glass goes from "small" to "large" with its short
  side h: t = (h - 44) / 116, from 0 to 1; lift 16 - 12 t, glow 0.3 - 0.1 t,
  wash 1 - t (the tab bar's platter, 62pt: 14.14pt, 0.2845, 0.845).
  Rise: the liquid lens's lift spring (stiffness 541.54, damping 29.09: 8%
  over at 0.17s). Release: the variant's scale spring (246.74 / 11.78, 28%
  under at 0.22s; to 304.62 / 20.94 for large glass).
  Wash: a white layer through a vibrant matrix (saturation 1.2, lift 0.05),
  the tab bar's. Glow: a white disk 1.5 h across (at most 160pt), blurred
  by 0.75 h, under the finger in the glass's own coordinates, through a
  vibrant matrix (2.73 x the color less 0.91 x its luma: grays x1.82). Both
  rise within 0.1s and fall over half a second; on release the glow
  spreads to 4x as it fades. A finger past the glass's edge: the glow stops
  at the edge, twice as large, at half its opacity.
  Drag: the glass stretches toward the finger, keeping its area, and moves
  a little that way. Liquidum's law is approximate: up to UIKit's 10%
  (its movement min 0.9, max 1.1), 0.1 tanh(d^2 / 20000) for a drag of d
  points, and 0.025 d (at most 3pt) toward the finger; fitted to four drags
  of 20 to 120pt on one button.

Open
  - Labels on dark glass are drawn with the same law, white; not compared.
  - The drag's stretch law is approximate (four drags).
  - Tap: the button fires on a tap as Flutter's (cancelled past the touch
    slop); UIKit's fires on release within about 70pt of the button.
  - Icons are the app's IconData, not SF symbols (UIKit's symbol box is
    kept, so layouts match).
