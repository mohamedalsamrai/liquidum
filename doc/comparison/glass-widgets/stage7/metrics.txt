UIKit's laws (traced every frame: the presented UIDropShadowView's
presentation layer, its layer dump and its archived glass group)
  Detents: the screen minus both safe areas is the maximum (860pt); medium
    is 0.56 of it (481.6pt), large all of it; a custom detent is its height.
    Rounded to the screen's pixels (481.67). The sheet's frame is the detent
    plus the bottom safe area (515.67, 894), its bottom at the screen's.
  Scale: the whole sheet (content included) is scaled about its middle, by
    1 - 16/W (0.9636) at and below the medium detent, so that it keeps 8pt
    off the sides and the bottom; between medium and large the scale grows
    with the frame's height to 1 at large. The inset at the sides and the
    bottom is always (1 - scale) W / 2. Medium on screen: 8, 451.08,
    424 x 496.92; large: 0, 62, 440 x 894.
  Corners (cornerRadii, before the scale): top 38; bottom 62 - 14 (1 - h /
    894) for a frame h tall (56.17 at medium, 53.29 at 334, the screen's 62
    at large): about concentric with the screen at medium.
  Glass: large regular glass with its fixed face (light: black 0.5, white
    1.03, white fill 0.4; dark: 0.2, 0.6, black fill 0.4), blur 4,
    refraction 60 at 20, shadow 0.25, radius 24, offset 8: Stage 1's large
    glass, but its bleed is off (opacity 0).
  Opaque: past halfway from the medium detent's frame to the large one's
    (its top crossing about y 257-263) a fill fades in, white or (28, 28,
    30) in the dark, on a critically damped spring (omega 18.27); it fades
    out the same way going down.
  Dim: black at 0.2 (0.48 in the dark) over the screen, with how far the
    sheet is in (presenting, dismissing) or how far it is from its lowest
    detent to gone (dragged). A tap on it dismisses the sheet.
  Present: from below the screen, critically damped, omega 18.1 (0.347s),
    starting about 0.08s after present(); the sheet comes in from the left
    of its place by its inset (its x drifts 0 -> 8 with the rise).
    Dismiss: the same spring (a drag let go: omega 15.4 with its speed).
  Drag: the sheet follows the finger 1:1; its frame grows above its lowest
    detent (with the scale), it slides down below it. Let go: to a detent,
    springing omega 12.9, damping 0.845 with the finger's speed, or away.
  Touch: the sheet is interactive glass: a finger on it swells it by 4pt
    (424 -> 428 wide, scale 0.9636 -> 0.9725) on Stage 3's springs; a fast
    drag stretches it along the drag (scale 0.944 across, 0.995 along at
    about 1500pt/s).

Liquidum
  An overlay route over the root navigator: the dim, then the sheet laid
  out at the screen's width and its frame's height (MediaQuery padding:
  the bottom safe area) and scaled. Glass._sheet: large glass without
  bleed and with its own bottom corner radius (glass shapes now carry one;
  shaders/glass.frag picks it below the shape's middle). Springs:
  GlassSpring, stepped by a ticker. A let-go drag goes to the detent
  nearest to where its speed would carry it (UIScrollView's projection,
  0.499s), or away.

Open

  - The drag's stretch (fast drags) and the press glow are not copied; the
    swell is.
  - The grabber (prefersGrabberVisible), largestUndimmedDetent, a keyboard,
    detents changed in code, and the page's tint dimming (UIKit grays the
    page's tint while a sheet is up) are not done.
  - The screen's corner radius is the 6.9" iPhone's 62pt on every screen.
  - Labels on UIKit's glass sheet are vibrant (vibrantColorMatrix); the
    content's text is drawn as it is.

    (1500pt/s): its rule for a let-go drag is not measured.
