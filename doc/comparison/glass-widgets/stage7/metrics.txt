Stage 7, presentations. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max
(440 x 956pt, safe area 62 / 34, 3 px/pt). Levels: 0-255, mean absolute
difference between UIKit's screenshot and Liquidum's (example/lib/
menus_comparison.dart `present`), unless RMS is said.

Part 1: the sheet (UISheetPresentationController), 2026-11-15
  showGlassSheet(context:, builder:, detents:) with GlassSheetDetent.medium,
  .large and .height(h). 1-sheet.jpg: UIKit above, Liquidum below; medium
  light, medium dark, large.

UIKit's laws (traced every frame: the presented UIDropShadowView's
presentation layer, its layer dump and its archived glass group)
  Detents: the screen minus both safe areas is the maximum (860pt); medium
    is 0.56 of it (481.6pt), large all of it; a custom detent is its height.
    Rounded to the screen's pixels (481.67). The sheet's frame is the detent
    plus the bottom safe area (515.67, 894), its bottom at the screen's.
  Scale: the whole sheet (content included) is scaled about its middle, by
    1 - 16/W (0.9636) at and below the medium detent, so that it keeps 8pt
    off the sides and the bottom; between medium and large the scale grows
    with the frame's height to 1 at large. The inset at the sides and the
    bottom is always (1 - scale) W / 2. Medium on screen: 8, 451.08,
    424 x 496.92; large: 0, 62, 440 x 894.
  Corners (cornerRadii, before the scale): top 38; bottom 62 - 14 (1 - h /
    894) for a frame h tall (56.17 at medium, 53.29 at 334, the screen's 62
    at large): about concentric with the screen at medium.
  Glass: large regular glass with its fixed face (light: black 0.5, white
    1.03, white fill 0.4; dark: 0.2, 0.6, black fill 0.4), blur 4,
    refraction 60 at 20, shadow 0.25, radius 24, offset 8: Stage 1's large
    glass, but its bleed is off (opacity 0).
  Opaque: past halfway from the medium detent's frame to the large one's
    (its top crossing about y 257-263) a fill fades in, white or (28, 28,
    30) in the dark, on a critically damped spring (omega 18.27); it fades
    out the same way going down.
  Dim: black at 0.2 (0.48 in the dark) over the screen, with how far the
    sheet is in (presenting, dismissing) or how far it is from its lowest
    detent to gone (dragged). A tap on it dismisses the sheet.
  Present: from below the screen, critically damped, omega 18.1 (0.347s),
    starting about 0.08s after present(); the sheet comes in from the left
    of its place by its inset (its x drifts 0 -> 8 with the rise).
    Dismiss: the same spring (a drag let go: omega 15.4 with its speed).
  Drag: the sheet follows the finger 1:1; its frame grows above its lowest
    detent (with the scale), it slides down below it. Let go: to a detent,
    springing omega 12.9, damping 0.845 with the finger's speed, or away.
  Touch: the sheet is interactive glass: a finger on it swells it by 4pt
    (424 -> 428 wide, scale 0.9636 -> 0.9725) on Stage 3's springs; a fast
    drag stretches it along the drag (scale 0.944 across, 0.995 along at
    about 1500pt/s).

Liquidum
  An overlay route over the root navigator: the dim, then the sheet laid
  out at the screen's width and its frame's height (MediaQuery padding:
  the bottom safe area) and scaled. Glass._sheet: large glass without
  bleed and with its own bottom corner radius (glass shapes now carry one;
  shaders/glass.frag picks it below the shape's middle). Springs:
  GlassSpring, stepped by a ticker. A let-go drag goes to the detent
  nearest to where its speed would carry it (UIScrollView's projection,
  0.499s), or away.

Results (screenshots, settled)
  medium, light: dimmed page 0.03; sheet middle 2.0 (RMS 2.9); edge band
    (12pt inside its outline) 3.8; whole sheet 2.3.
  medium, dark: dimmed page 0.02; middle 1.4; edge 3.4; label strip 2.5;
    whole sheet 1.7.
  large, light: middle 0.00; edge 0.7; whole sheet 0.1.
  Rise: Liquidum's curve against four UIKit traces, start aligned: RMS
    0.4-0.9pt, 2.5-5.4pt at most (the first frame, about a frame of phase).
  Widget tests (test/glass_sheet_test.dart): medium, large, a custom
  detent within 0.05pt of UIKit's frames; the rise 0.1s in.

Open
  - Scroll views in the sheet take its vertical drags: UIKit's sheet grows
    when its content is scrolled at its top edge; Liquidum's does not yet.
  - The drag's stretch (fast drags) and the press glow are not copied; the
    swell is.
  - The grabber (prefersGrabberVisible), largestUndimmedDetent, a keyboard,
    detents changed in code, and the page's tint dimming (UIKit grays the
    page's tint while a sheet is up) are not done.
  - The screen's corner radius is the 6.9" iPhone's 62pt on every screen.
  - Labels on UIKit's glass sheet are vibrant (vibrantColorMatrix); the
    content's text is drawn as it is.
  - UIKit picked no detent above medium for XCUITest's fast upward drag
    (1500pt/s): its rule for a let-go drag is not measured.

Part 2: the alert and action sheet (UIAlertController), 2026-11-15

  GlassAlertAction(label:, onPressed:, destructive:, cancel:) and

UIKit's laws (layer dumps, archived glass group, traced every frame)
  Place: 320pt wide, in the middle of the safe area; corners 34. Glass:
    Stage 1's large regular glass, with its bleed (0.5 light, 0.8 dark).
    Dim: a plain black view at 0.2 (0.48 dark); a tap on it does nothing.
  Text: title 17 semibold, 22pt down, 30pt in, leading; message 15 regular
    7.4pt below it; lines 22 and 20pt apart (the first and last keep the
    font's height: 20.3, 18). A title or a message alone: 17 regular,
    centered, 21.7pt down. Then 4.3pt (4 alone) + 16 to the actions.
    Colors (vibrant on the glass; measured over grays, light / dark): title
    black / white 0.95, message black 0.548 / white 0.6.
  Actions: capsules 48pt tall, 16pt in from the sides and the bottom, 8pt
    apart. Fill: black at 0.12 / white at 0.108 (over the glass: 0.86-0.88
    times it, no hue change, RMS 0.6 over blocks). Labels 17 medium (w500,
    by ink and width against SF's weights), black / white, red FF383C /
    FF4245. Two side by side (140pt each), the cancel action left; one or
    more than two stacked, the cancel action last. A pressed capsule's fill
    at 0.4. A finger can slide to another action before it lifts.
  Text fields: 48pt rows with the actions' fill, 15pt in, 12pt below the
    text, one rounded box (24pt corners) with a 1pt line between rows
    (15pt in; black 0.25 / white 0.15); text 17 regular 15pt in; placeholder
    (60, 60, 67, 0.3) / (235, 235, 245, 0.3); cursor (0.26, 0.42, 0.95),
    2 x 23. The first is focused. Then 4 + 16pt to the actions.
  Motion: opacity 0 -> 1 and scale 1.2 -> 1 together on one critically
    damped spring, omega 22.9 (fitted RMS 0.0004 / 0.0001); dismissed:
    opacity only, the same spring. The dim eases out (cubic) over 0.275s
    both ways (RMS 0.01). A finger on the alert swells it by 4pt (Stage 3's
    large glass: 1.0125 after an 8% overshoot).
  Action sheet: with no view to present from, iOS 26.5 shows it exactly as
    the alert (layout, glass, springs), but a tap outside dismisses it. With
    one (popoverPresentationController.sourceView) it is a 240pt popover by
    that view, without its cancel action: part 3.

Large glass's bleed (refitted)
  UIKit's bleed, as Stage 1 found, is linear in its opacity; here it was
  captured with opacity 0 and 1 (kvc) behind the alert over blocks, white,
  gray and black, and (dark) behind 360pt panels over an undimmed screen.
  Over grays it is not a matrix of the glass Stage 1's blocks-only fit
  could follow: light, min(G + 12.5, 241) levels; dark, it darkens more
  over darker backdrops (30 -> 12 over black). Now: light, an affine
  matrix of the glass capped at 0.948; dark, an affine matrix plus -2.07
  times the glass's luma squared (shaders/glass.frag uBleed[3].y, .z).
  Against UIKit's own glass, final RMS: light 0.6-2.5, dark 0.5-2.6
  (Stage 1's fit: light 1.8-4.0, dark 1.7-17).
  Stage 1's panels (360 x 200 / 250 / 300, comparison app `big`), middle,
  HEAD -> now: light blocks 3.7-3.8 -> 3.7-3.8; light white 3.0-3.2 ->
  2.8-3.0; light black 1.3-1.8 -> 0.7-1.2; dark blocks 3.1-3.6 -> 3.7-4.0;
  dark white 2.5-4.0 -> 0.7-1.3; dark black 1.7-2.4 -> 0.3-1.0.

Liquidum
  An overlay route over the root navigator (as the sheet's): the dim, then
  the alert, a Glass(radius: 34, interactive: true) under GlassPresence
  (its glass materializes with the spring; its content's colors fade with

Open

    (over strongly colored glass they would differ a little).
  - When two actions stop fitting side by side (estimated: a label wider
    than 116pt) is not measured; nor UIKit's preferredAction (bold), the
    keyboard's effect on the alert's place (the simulator's hardware
    keyboard hides it), or an alert taller than the screen (its text
    scrolls here).
  - The action sheet presented from a view (a popover): part 3.
  - The 0.2s UIKit waits before its alert appears in the catalog is not
    copied (likely its first layout).
  - Liquidum's alert has not been compared on Android.
