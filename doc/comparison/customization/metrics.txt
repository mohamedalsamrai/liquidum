Tab customization, 2026-10-13. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max.
Images: UIKit above, Liquidum below; tint #FF3366, badges "5" and "99+".

UIKit's behaviour (read from its views and pixels over white, #808080, black):
  tintColor: selected symbols and labels = 0.5 * glass below + tint - 0.5
    (light appearance), + tint (dark). Checked with the default blue
    (light 0,136,255 / dark 0,145,255), #FF3366 and #33CC66: within 1 level.
  unselectedItemTintColor and UITabBarAppearance's normal colors: ignored by
    the floating iOS 26 bar (normal items stay adaptive).
  badges: drawn as they are (light 255,56,60 / dark 255,66,69, by the bar's
    appearance), 20.33pt high, at least as wide, 4pt beside the text, white
    SF 12 medium, 6pt below the bar's top, 5pt over the symbol's trailing
    edge (leading edge right to left); in both content views, so the lens
    lifts them with the selected tab.
  images in their original rendering mode: drawn as they are.
  labels: selected semibold, normal medium (Liquidum had semibold for both).
  right to left: tab order mirrored.

Liquidum vs UIKit:
  selected interior color: (245,41,92) / (218,14,65) / (255,77,128) on
    white / gray / black: identical to UIKit.
  badge position: within 0.33pt; "99+" 1pt narrower (Flutter's text is
    narrower and a little lighter than UIKit's).
  normal labels, medium vs semibold, mean error over New/Radio: 3.5/2.6 vs
    4.6/4.8 levels (white).
  whole bar at rest, default colors: mean 1.2-1.3 levels; tinted with
    badges: 1.9-2.2 (glyph edges).
  lifted at the first tab (4-lifted-tint-badge): mean 3.1, p95 10 levels.

Glass tints (UIGlassEffect.tintColor), 2026-10-13. UIKit's tab bar has no
glass tint; liquidum's glassColor, indicatorColor and lensColor follow how
iOS tints glass, read from UIGlassEffect views (5-uikit-tinted-glass: plain,
#FF3366 and #33CC66 regular glass, then clear glass, then both at 50%).
  The glass filter is unchanged; a layer inside it applies a backdrop-aware
  color matrix to the glass: rows proportional to Rec. 709 luma, so the
  glass's luma L maps to dark + (bright - dark) L, unclamped, then mixed in
  at the tint's alpha (50% tints: within 1 level of the mix).
  Model vs UIKit pixels over a black-white ramp, regular and clear glass:
  0 levels at 7 points each.
  The same matrix over black and white backgrounds and for regular and clear
  glass; different in the dark appearance (system dark mode).
  bright = the tint itself (light appearance); exact grays apart: 0.95 g over
  black glass, 0.1 + 0.9 g over white (a gray 1/255 off is chromatic).
  dark (and dark-appearance bright) has no simple form found (not an HSL,
  OKLab or affine map of the tint); it is continuous and symmetric under
  swapping channels, so it was measured on a 9-level grid of sorted tints
  (165 per appearance; tint-light.txt, tint-dark.txt: tint, then the matrix's
  luma weight per channel and offset) and is interpolated tetrahedrally.
  Interpolation vs 8 off-grid tints: max 0.004 except #33CC66 (0.03 over
  black glass, 0 over white).
Liquidum (6-liquidum-glass-tints: red glass with a green resting selection;
a blue lens, held), each against the model applied to its untinted glass:
  platter, light and dark, full and 50%: within 1 level
  lens, lifted: 0 levels
  resting selection: within 4 levels
