Tab customization, 2026-10-13. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max.
Images: UIKit above, Liquidum below; tint #FF3366, badges "5" and "99+".

UIKit's behaviour (read from its views and pixels over white, #808080, black):
  tintColor: selected symbols and labels = 0.5 * glass below + tint - 0.5
    (light appearance), + tint (dark). Checked with the default blue
    (light 0,136,255 / dark 0,145,255), #FF3366 and #33CC66: within 1 level.
  unselectedItemTintColor and UITabBarAppearance's normal colors: ignored by
    the floating iOS 26 bar (normal items stay adaptive).
  badges: drawn as they are (light 255,56,60 / dark 255,66,69, by the bar's
    appearance), 20.33pt high, at least as wide, 4pt beside the text, white
    SF 12 medium, 6pt below the bar's top, 5pt over the symbol's trailing
    edge (leading edge right to left); in both content views, so the lens
    lifts them with the selected tab.
  images in their original rendering mode: drawn as they are.
  labels: selected semibold, normal medium (Liquidum had semibold for both).
  right to left: tab order mirrored.

Liquidum vs UIKit:
  selected interior color: (245,41,92) / (218,14,65) / (255,77,128) on
    white / gray / black: identical to UIKit.
  badge position: within 0.33pt; "99+" 1pt narrower (Flutter's text is
    narrower and a little lighter than UIKit's).
  normal labels, medium vs semibold, mean error over New/Radio: 3.5/2.6 vs
    4.6/4.8 levels (white).
  whole bar at rest, default colors: mean 1.2-1.3 levels; tinted with
    badges: 1.9-2.2 (glyph edges).
  lifted at the first tab (4-lifted-tint-badge): mean 3.1, p95 10 levels.
