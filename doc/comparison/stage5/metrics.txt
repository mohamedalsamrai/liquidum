Stage 5 (lighting), 2026-10-13. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max.
Each part of UIKit's lighting was read from its layers, rebuilt alone from the
archived filters over plain backgrounds, fitted, then compared with the real bar.

Rim highlight (CASDFKeyFillHighlightEffect; platter curvature 0.7, lens 0.8)
  model: alpha = |n . (0.707, 0.707)| * (1 - curvature * depth/1pt), within 1pt
  fit over 10x renders: mean error 1.4 levels; at the real 1pt rim, the rim's
  sum per angle within 1-4% of UIKit's, mean pixel error 3.5 levels (platter).
  compositing (vibrant color matrix): mix(below, M below, alpha), M = 1.5 chroma
  + luma weight + lift (platter 0.1, 0.9; lens 0.4374, 1.125); within 1 level.

Shadows (glassBackground), outside the glass:
  below * (1 - strength * erfc-edge of the outline moved down)
  platter: offset 8pt, sigma 16.4pt (0.685 * radius 24); fit 0.27 levels
  lens:    offset 7pt, sigma 5.49pt (0.685 * radius 8), strength 0.1086; fit 0.31 levels
  strength = opacity * (0.093 + 0.966 * fill); linear in both (checked at 5 values)
  the platter's fill adapts to the backdrop's luma: 0.3 up to 0.385, -0.224 per
  unit above, 0.05 over L > 0.935 (sweep over 16 flat backgrounds)
  the shadow multiplies every channel alike (saturation input has no effect)

Press: wash (white layer, vibrant matrix: chroma x1.2, +0.05, at 0.8448) and
glow (white disk 93pt, shadow-blurred sigma 46.5pt, 0.2845), over the platter
and its tabs, under the lens; the lens's 0.97 tone applies after them.
  wash: platter 205 -> 216 on gray (model 215.8), (23,25,28) -> (33,36,39) on dark
  glow near the finger: 217.6 vs 217.4 (gray), (41.9,44.9,47.9) vs (42.5,44.8,48.2) (dark)
  lens interior over flat grays, 0.97 x (platter + wash + glow): 241.3/240.6,
  228.5/228.6, 197.4/197.1, 247.4/246.9 (model/UIKit)
  springs: press up 0.181s/0.865, down 0.499s/1.0, spread 0.53s/0.96
  (max error 0.01); same-gesture comparison of press, glow size and opacity
  within the run-to-run timing of the two tests.

Liquidum vs UIKit, white background (the platter face matches there):
  rest: shadows above/below/beside the platter, rims, pill: all within 1 level
  lifted: platter, its shadow, the lens's shadow and interior: within 1 level
  remaining: a band 3-14pt inside the lens's top edge, darker in UIKit by up
  to 3.2% (9 levels on white, 3-4 on gray); cause not found (4-lens-top-band).

Not Stage 5 (Stage 6): the platter's face color matrix adapts to the backdrop
(e.g. 205 vs 236 on gray), the light/dark appearance switch below L ~0.2, the
icons' vibrant colors. On gray and blocks these dominate the differences.
