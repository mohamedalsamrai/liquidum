Refinements after the six stages, 2026-10-16. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator,
iPhone 17 Pro Max, 3 px/pt. RMS is in luma levels (0-255) against UIKit.

Frost (platter glass): blurred once, then refracted
  UIKit's glassBackground mixes its heavy blur into its light filtering by
  the depth of the point it samples (after refraction), not the point it
  draws: mixing by the sample's depth halves the error in the refracted edge
  bands (stripes, rows 2620-2645 and 2780-2806: 7.5/8.1 -> 3.9/4.4).
  So the frost is now drawn before the glass, into the backdrop under the
  platter: a native blur (Impeller, sigma 2.9pt) and over it this shader's
  light blur (sigma 0.46pt) at 0.5 - 0.5 t^0.72 of the way in (t the depth
  from 1pt to 31pt), both from one capture of their own. The glass passes
  then read the frost from their capture, filtered (the engine samples it at
  the nearest pixel), and refract it.
  Fit (stripes, middle rows, face gain fixed at the shader's 0.296): light
  0.46pt, heavy 2.91pt, ramp power 0.72, rms 0.59 levels.
  Whole platter, between the glyphs (edge top | middle | edge bottom | all):
    hstripe8  before 13.04 3.15  8.80 6.39   after 10.71 1.86  4.51 4.56
    vstripe8  before 15.42 4.33 16.38 9.11   after 11.43 2.68 12.92 6.79
    grid      before  7.39 3.58  7.58 4.97   after  5.50 2.04  6.06 3.49
    blocks    before  4.65 1.15  5.05 2.73   after  4.60 1.05  4.93 2.65
    saw       before  8.62 2.68 11.60 5.83   after  8.45 2.69 11.56 5.79
  The sparse in-shader blur ghosted fine lines (1-frost-uikit-before-after:
  UIKit, before, after, the resting pill and the lifted glyph over a grid).
  On Android (Impeller GLES) the frost lines up with the backdrop within
  0.65px.

Sampling
  Capture reads are filtered between the four nearest pixels; the content
  atlas is sampled with FilterQuality.low.

Speed (honest): the frost needs a second backdrop capture.
  Android emulator, host GPU, 6 bars, raster: before 22 ms, after 30 ms (it
  counts render passes, not shader work: holding the lens changed nothing
  before). Software GPU, 1 bar: inconclusive. Texture reads per pixel at
  rest: platter 78 -> about 10, lens 110 -> about 30; lifted lens about 550
  -> 170. Whether that outweighs the extra capture on a real phone is not
  measured yet.

Lens top band
  UIKit's lifted lens is darker inside its top edge by a constant 3.3% over
  white, gray and a position code (all sample the same backdrop row there),
  fading over 15pt: the complement of its shadow's edge, at the point its
  material reads (its glassBackground's inputSDRShadowOpacity, 0.111, is
  the likely source). Modeled as 1 - 0.06 (1 - shadow edge).
  Band rows 2609-2650, straight part: white rms 5.12 -> 1.23, gray 3.68 ->
  2.92 (2-lens-top-band: UIKit, before, after; white, then gray).

Search tab, bottom accessory, minimizing (3-search-accessory-minimize)
  From UIKit's view frames every frame:
  - search tab: a 62pt circle at the trailing end, 8pt from the platter;
    the platter at the leading end; with a search tab, 2 tabs hug (188pt),
    3 and 4 fill (328pt), pills 113.33 and 85.5pt (overlap 20 and 22pt).
  - accessory: 48pt high, 8pt above the bar, the bar's width.
  - minimized: 48pt circles 7pt in from the row's ends, the accessory
    between them 8pt from each, all on the row's middle line.
  - springs (response s, damping): platter size 0.591/0.861, its center
    0.393/0.862; accessory span 0.36/0.72 from +0.10s, drop 0.42/0.76 from
    +0.15s; expanding, its rise 0.30/0.72 at once and span from +0.067s.
    Each fits UIKit's within 0.3% of the move.

Scroll-edge effect
  None behind UIKit's tab bar: stripes as cell backgrounds or as cell
  content keep black at 0 above and below the bar; white only darkens by
  the platter's shadow.

Accessibility (4-accessibility: UIKit above Liquidum, then Reduce Motion)
  Increase Contrast (UIKit's filter inputs): face fill white 0.8, face
  saturation 1.1 -> 0.8, shadow fill 0.12; tabs drawn as they are; the
  selected color fixed: system blue #1E6EF4 (dark #5CB8FF), other colors
  in OKLab L x0.796, C x0.82 (light; within 3 levels), 0.788 L + 0.274,
  C x0.894 (dark; within 13 levels).
    over blocks rms 5.48, over white 1.02.
  Reduce Transparency: no refraction, blur radius 0.67 -> 36.9 fully,
  face luma 0.9 flat, saturation 0.9, fill white 0.8.
    over blocks rms 5.91, over white 1.99 (white: 242 in both).
  Reduce Motion: the resting pill slides under the finger, never lifts or
  flexes (UIKit's lens stays 107.5 x 54 throughout a drag).

Minimizing, closer (5-minimize-crossfade: UIKit left, Liquidum right, the
same 33ms steps)
  UIKit crossfades two snapshots (_UIMagicMorphTransformView): the tabs go
  out, opacity 1 - p and gaussianBlur radius 4p, the minimized circle's icon
  comes in the reverse; p is a spring, response 0.255s, damping 0.86 (fits
  within 0.4%), from 0.09s after the selection starts collapsing. The tabs
  stay where they are, clipped by the shrinking glass; the selected icon
  slides into the circle. The platter's own shape collapses faster than its
  selection: width 0.353/0.772, height 0.44/0.78, center 0.311/0.828
  (expanding: 0.295/0.811, 0.45/0.81, 0.30/0.80). Radius 4pt is taken as a
  3.3pt sigma (the resting selection's radius 2 at quarter scale fitted
  2.22pt).

Search field (6-search-field: the opening at 33ms steps, then open and
with the keyboard, UIKit above Liquidum)
  With a search controller on the search tab's page, UIKit opens the bar into
  _UITabHostedSearchContainer: the tabs collapse into the 48pt circle (the
  last tab's icon, in the normal color; a tap goes back), the search circle
  grows into a 48pt field from 12pt after the circle to 7pt from the row's
  end (88 to 412pt on the reference screen), in about 0.25s. Its magnifying
  glass (17.5pt, 1.5pt strokes) sits 13pt in and its placeholder, "Search"
  at 17pt, 41.7pt in. With the keyboard the field ends 12pt short of a 48pt
  close button (264pt wide); the close button ends the editing. Pressed, the
  search circle swells to 79pt.
