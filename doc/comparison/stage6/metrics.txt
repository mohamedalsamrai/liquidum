Stage 6 (adaptive appearance), 2026-10-13. UIKit = iOS 26.5 simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max.
UIKit's adaptive inputs were logged every frame from its tab bar's filters while the
comparison app changed the background (flat grays, steps, rectangles), with marks
for when each change was shown.

What UIKit reads: the mean luma (Rec. 709 on the colors) of the backdrop over the
bar's container grown by the glass's 12.4pt margin (here x 0..440, y 860.6..956),
read about every frame. The glass's inputs use it rounded to 1/32; the light/dark
switch uses it as is. (Probes: black under the platter on white 13/32, white
under it on gray 25/32, left half 11/32, a band above 14/32, below 12/32, beside
15/32; Liquidum's face black within 0.0003 of UIKit's on all of them.)

Switch (hysteresis): system light: dark below 0.210, light above 0.492.
                     system dark:  light above 0.867, dark below 0.584.
  Liquidum switches at the same steps (0x35 / 0x7e; 0xde / 0x94).
